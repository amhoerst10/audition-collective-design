What This Is

The Audition Collective is a curated, centralized resource of upcoming auditions for the modern-day musician.

Log in with a free account today and start your search as a career musician. All group submissions are vetted to ensure they publicize their auditions openly, and that they pay for your services.

● Free with a free account — no payment required.
